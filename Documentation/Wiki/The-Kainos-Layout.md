# The Kainos Layout

Kainos opens in the **Kainos layout**. You can switch back to Thetis's own look in **Setup > Appearance > Kainos > Layout** (Classic), and change the size of Kainos's controls with **UI scale** (75 to 200%).

## The left-hand column

Power, transmit and VFO buttons: **POWER**, **RX2**, **MOX**, **TUN**, **2TON**, **SWR** (see [[SWR Sweep]]), **MON**, **VOX**, **DUP**, **PS-A**, **SPLT**, **A>B**, **A<B**, **A<>B**, **0 Beat**, **IF->V**, **REC**, **PLAY**, **VAC1** and **VAC2**. Right-click a button for the same settings shortcut as in Thetis.

## The panadapter

- **VFO flags** show each receiver's antenna, filter, DSP, mode, frequency and S meter. See [[VFO Flags]].
- **The band plan** along the bottom shows what each part of the band is for, and your privileges. See [[Band Plan]].
- **Spots** from the DX cluster and POTA appear as callsign tags. See [[Spots]].
- **The bar under the panadapter** has pan, zoom, the display mode, AVG / Peak / CTUN, multi-RX pan, and **3D**.

### The 3D panadapter (experimental)

Press **3D** under the panadapter to stack the last few seconds of traces behind the live one, in the waterfall's colours. Right-click it for its settings. It uses more CPU; on a slower PC use [[light mode|Backups and Updates]].

### The panadapter background

**Setup > Appearance > Kainos > Panadapter:** the Kainos navy background with the logo faint behind the trace (and how strong the logo is), Kainos's panadapter colours, and how see-through the VFO flags are.

## The right-hand column

A column of tabs you turn on and off by clicking them: **VFO**, **VFO SYNC**, **METERS**, **BAND**, **RX**, **TX**, **PA**, **EQ**, **KAINOS AUDIO**, **FREEDV**, **MEMORY**, **SPOTS** and **KIWI**. See [[The Right-Hand Column]].

## Menus

The menu bar has Kainos's windows (**Kainos Audio**, **FreeDV**, **RTTY**, **CW** ...) next to Thetis's. **Report a bug** is at the top right. The **Setup** menu also has **Back up settings now**, **Run setup wizard**, **Light mode**, **Check for updates** and **Report a bug**.
