# Backups, Updates and Bug Reports


## Back up your settings

**Setup > Back up settings now** backs up your current settings with one click.

To **restore** a backup, or keep several **settings profiles** (say, home and portable), use **Setup > Database Manager**.

## Updates

Once a day Kainos checks for a new version and, if there is one, shows what's new with **Download**, **Later** and **Skip this version**. **Setup > Check for updates** checks right away.

## Report a bug

**Report a bug** is at the top right of the menu bar (and in the Setup menu).

1. Write a short summary and what happened (steps to make it happen again help a lot).
2. Kainos adds **diagnostics**: versions, Windows, screen, layout, radio, audio and recent errors.
3. **Open on GitHub** opens a new issue in your browser with it all filled in. Nothing is sent until you press **Submit** there (a free GitHub account is needed). **Copy to clipboard** lets you paste it elsewhere instead.

## Light mode

For slower PCs: **Light mode** turns the 3D panadapter off and lowers the display to 20 frames a second. Find it in the **Setup** menu and **Setup > Appearance > Kainos**; the setup wizard suggests it on PCs with 4 or fewer processor threads. Turning it off puts your frame rate and 3D back.
