# VFO Flags

Each receiver has a **flag** on the panadapter, SmartSDR style: the antenna, filter, active DSP, mode, frequency and an S meter, and a **TX** marker on the transmit VFO.

## Tuning

- **Mouse wheel over a digit:** tunes by that digit.
- **Mouse wheel elsewhere:** tunes by your tune step.
- **Click the frequency:** type one, then press Enter (Esc cancels).
- **Click TX** on the other VFO: make it the transmit VFO.

## The tabs under the flag

**AUDIO**, **DSP**, **MODE**, **RIT/XIT**, **VAC** and **FREEDV** open a drawer under the flag with those controls. Click the tab again to close it.

## Keeping the flags out of the way

- **See-through:** flags are see-through until you point at them, so spots and markers behind them show. Set how see-through in **Setup > Appearance > Kainos > Slice flags**.
- **Drag** a flag by its face to move it down the panadapter; **double-click** the face to put it back at the top.
- **One line:** click the **A** or **B** on a flag to shrink it to one line (mode, frequency and TX). Click it again for the full flag.

## Split

With **SPLT** on (and RX2 off), the VFO B flag shows **SPLIT** (or **QUICK SPLIT**) and sits at VFO B's frequency on the panadapter.
