# Spots

DX cluster and POTA spots, built in, with no other program.

- **DX cluster:** Kainos connects to NC7J's cluster and logs in with your callsign automatically (the callsign from the setup wizard, also in **Setup > DSP > FreeDV (RADE)**). It reconnects by itself if the connection drops.
- **POTA:** activators currently on the air, from POTA's spot feed, checked every two minutes.

## Where they show

- **On the panadapter:** callsign tags with a flag. Click one to tune to it.
- **The SPOTS tab** in the right-hand column: the latest spots. Click a row to tune; **Band** shows only your current band.
- **On the KiwiSDR view** too. See [[KiwiSDR]].
