# Getting Started

## The first start

If Thetis is on the same PC, Kainos asks how you'd like to start:

- **Set up for my Hermes Lite 2** (recommended): fresh settings and a few questions.
- **Import my Thetis settings:** copies your Thetis databases, meters, skins and cmASIO settings (Thetis isn't changed), then asks the same questions. Close Thetis first.
- **Skip:** Kainos's default settings.

## The setup wizard

The wizard sets Kainos up for your HL2. Nothing changes until you press **Apply** on the last page. It's offered once after installing or updating, and you can run it again any time from **Setup > Appearance > Kainos > Run setup wizard** or the **Setup** menu.

| Page | What it asks |
|---|---|
| **Station** | Your callsign and grid square (used for spots, FreeDV RADE and the FreeDV Reporter), your country and licence class (used by the [[Band Plan]]). |
| **Hardware** | Which boards your HL2 has: the N2ADR filter board, the HL2 I/O board, the built-in PA, and band data for an external amplifier. Clock and latency options are under *Show advanced options*. |
| **Audio** | The HL2 has no speaker output of its own, so receive audio and your microphone go through the PC. Pick your speakers and microphone, and press **Play a test tone**. |
| **Look** | Kainos or Classic layout, UI scale, light mode, and which right-hand column tabs start open. |
| **Summary** | Exactly what will change. Press **Apply**. |

## Connecting to the radio

Press **POWER** at the top of the left-hand column. If Kainos doesn't find your HL2, see **Setup > General > H/W Select** (Discover) and [[Troubleshooting and FAQ]].
