# Band Plan

A band along the bottom of the panadapter shows, at a glance, **what each part of the band is used for** and **where you may transmit**.

## What it shows

- **Labels:** CW, DIGITAL, SSB, BEACONS, AM, SATELLITE or FM, with a divider where each section starts. From the ARRL band plan in the Americas, and the IARU Region 1 plan elsewhere.
- **Tags above it** at the popular frequencies: FT8, FT4, WSPR, PSK31, SSTV, AM and QRP.
- **Colours** for your licence:

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Gold | All modes (phone too) |
| Ice blue | CW and data |
| Violet | CW only |
| Red | **Out of privileges**: in the band, but not yours (labelled so) |
| Grey | Band edges only (countries without built-in privileges) |

## Setting it up

The band plan uses the **country and licence class** from the setup wizard. You can change them, or turn the band plan off, in **Setup > Appearance > Kainos > Band plan**.

Privileges are built in for:

- **The United States:** Technician, General and Amateur Extra, 160 to 10 m and 6 m (60 m's channels are left out).
- **Canada:** Basic (6 m on the HL2), Basic with Honours and Advanced.

Other countries show the band edges only.

**It's a guide.** Band plans are voluntary and simplified here; always check your own licence before transmitting.
