# RTTY and CW

Built-in RTTY and CW, with no extra programs or virtual audio cables.

## RTTY

Open **Modes > RTTY** in the menu bar (**RTTY** on its own in the Classic layout). The terminal opens under the panadapter (the panadapter makes room), or in its own window with **Pop out**.

- Received text, type-ahead sending, a tuning indicator and **macros** you can edit.
- 45.45 baud, 170 Hz shift and others.
- Mark and space markers on the panadapter, with auto tune.
- Use **DIGL** or **LSB**.

## CW

Open **Modes > CW** in the menu bar (**CW** on its own in the Classic layout). The terminal is laid out like RTTY's.

- Decodes the receiver at your CW pitch and follows the sender's speed.
- What you type or send from the **macros** goes out through Thetis's CWX keyer at your chosen speed.
- Use **CWL** or **CWU**, and zero-beat the signal.
