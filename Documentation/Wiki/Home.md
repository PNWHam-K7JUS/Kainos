# Kainos

**Kainos** is a free, open-source SDR program for Windows, built for the **Hermes Lite 2**. It's a fork of [Thetis](https://github.com/ramdor/Thetis), based on Reid Campbell MI0BOT's [HL2 edition of Thetis](https://github.com/mi0bot/OpenHPSDR-Thetis), maintained by Justin Cron, K7JUS.

Everything you know from Thetis is still there. Kainos adds a modern layout and a set of features built in, so you don't need extra programs or virtual audio cables for them.

## What Kainos adds

- **A modern layout:** VFO flags on the panadapter, a right-hand column of tabs you arrange yourself, and a left-hand column of buttons. See [[The Kainos Layout]].
- **A guided setup** for the HL2: callsign, boards, audio and look in a few steps. See [[Getting Started]].
- **Kainos Audio:** EQ, compressor, AetherVoice and more on receive and transmit. See [[Kainos Audio]].
- **FreeDV RADE** digital voice, with the FreeDV Reporter. See [[FreeDV RADE]].
- **RTTY and CW** decode and send, with macros. See [[RTTY and CW]].
- **DX cluster and POTA spots** on the panadapter. See [[Spots]].
- **KiwiSDR:** listen to receivers around the world, with their own panadapter and waterfall, even with the HL2 off. See [[KiwiSDR]].
- **A licence-aware band plan** on the panadapter. See [[Band Plan]].
- **An SWR sweep** for your antennas. See [[SWR Sweep]].
- **Safety nets:** one-click settings backup, update notices and a bug-report button. See [[Backups and Updates]].
