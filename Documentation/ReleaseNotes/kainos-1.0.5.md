# Kainos 1.0.5

An update to [Kainos 1.0.0](https://github.com/PNWHam-K7JUS/Kainos/releases/tag/kainos-1.0.0), based on Thetis 2.10.3.15. See the 1.0.0 notes for everything Kainos adds.

## Changed

- **A cleaner TX tab in voice modes.** In the right-hand column:
  - **Mic, Comp and VOX** (and DEXP when shown) are full-width sliders with their value on the right, like Master AF. VOX keeps its level bar.
  - **Transmit Profile** is a full-width drop-down right under the sliders (the AM profile list in AM and SAM), with the TX filter's Low and High on one row.
  - **The buttons** (MIC, COMP, VOX, DEXP, RX EQ, TX EQ, AV, TX FL) are in two rows of four.
- **Rounded buttons:** the TX tab's buttons and the squelch bar now have the same rounded look as the rest of Kainos, gold while on.

The Classic layout is unchanged.

## Installing

Run **Kainos-1.0.5-x64.msi**. It replaces any earlier Kainos version and keeps your settings. Kainos installs alongside Thetis and doesn't change it.
