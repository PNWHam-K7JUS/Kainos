# Kainos 1.0.1

A small update to [Kainos 1.0.0](https://github.com/PNWHam-K7JUS/Kainos/releases/tag/kainos-1.0.0), based on Thetis 2.10.3.15. See the 1.0.0 notes for everything Kainos adds.

## Fixed

- **The squelch bar** in the right column's RX tab showed the skin's picture squashed to the column's width. It is now a clean Kainos bar ("SQL: 0").
- **The transmit panel's buttons** (MIC, COMP, VOX, DEXP, RX EQ, TX EQ, AV, TX FL) were the skin's small pictures, with labels cut off. They are now flat Kainos buttons, gold while on, with labels that fit. The skin's look is unchanged in the Classic layout.

## Installing

Run **Kainos-1.0.1-x64.msi**. It replaces Kainos 1.0.0 (or any earlier Kainos build) and keeps your settings. Kainos installs alongside Thetis and doesn't change it.
